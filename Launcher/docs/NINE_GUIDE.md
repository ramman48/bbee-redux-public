# Nine the Phantom: player guide

This guide matches version **0.9.0**. Names are the ones the game shows in English.

Nine is a caster. Her attacks and Skills are spells of **fire**, **wind** and **ice**. Each one leaves its element with her, and **Summon** weaves the elements she holds into a bigger spell. Build elements, weave, repeat.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| HP / Attack | 950 / 170 |
| MP | 100. Skills cost MP and have no cooldown |
| SP | fills as she spends MP. Her three SP moves each need their Potential |
| Dash | her **Blink**: she vanishes and reappears |
| Talent | Elemental Resonance |
| Legacy skills she passes on | Rubellite of Rage, Tanzanite of Torment |

"Up + Attack" means hold the direction while you press the button. "Forward" is the way she faces. Your own key or pad bindings apply.

## Elements

She holds up to **3 elements**, shown as orbs around her.

| What you do | What she gets |
|---|---|
| Any attack | **1** of its element |
| Any Skill | **2** of its element (Cobalt Hail gives 1 fire and 1 ice) |
| Cardinal Nova | one each of ice, wind and fire |

- **First in, first out.** When she already holds 3, a new element pushes out the oldest one.
- A move gives its elements once, a moment after it starts. Its hits and explosions don't add more.
- A Weave spends the elements it uses.

|---|---|---|

## Magic Arts

Press **Attack + Skill together** to switch Magic Arts on. Press them together again to switch back. It works on the ground and in the air, and the switch keeps the elements she holds.

While Magic Arts is on, her Attack string becomes her close-range strikes from BlazBlue Centralfiction:

| Input | Strikes | Elements |
|---|---|---|
| Attack ×3 (on the ground) | ice, wind, fire | ice, wind, fire |
| Down + Attack ×3 (on the ground) | wind, ice, fire | wind, ice, fire |
| Attack ×3 (in the air) | fire, ice, wind | fire, ice, wind |

- Each strike gives its element, so one ground string gives one of each: the recipe for Nephrite of Nullification.
- Her Skills and her Summon moves stay the same while Magic Arts is on.
- Press the two buttons at the same moment. If they are too far apart, you get the normal Attack or Skill instead.

## SP moves

SP fills as she spends MP. Each SP move is unlocked by the Potential of the same name.

| Input | Move | SP | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up + Summon | **Navy Pressure** (her Ultra) | 100 | the Phantom's giant arm strikes the nearest enemy ahead |
| Down + Skill | **Colorless Void** | 100 | a circle binds the nearest enemy ahead, then eight meteors fall on it. Invincible while casting |
| Forward + Skill | **Flame Punisher** | 50 | a torrent of flame from her palm, then a spinning kick fires a second, larger torrent. Invincible until she recovers |

Without enough SP, Down + Skill is Ultramarine March as usual.

## Talent: Elemental Resonance

After using a Legacy skill (or, as Nine, a Weave or Cardinal Nova), Skill damage increases by 25% for 8 seconds.

Other characters inherit it from her Evotype, like any Evotype talent.

## Her Evotype: Legacy skills

Finish a run with Nine and she becomes an Evotype. A character who picks it can use one of these, chosen on the Evotype screen:

| Legacy skill | Effect | Grows with level |
|---|---|---|
| **Rubellite of Rage** | fire meteors fall on the nearest enemies ahead | damage |
| **Tanzanite of Torment** | ice bursts erupt around you and freeze enemies | damage |

Nine herself can use the Legacy skills of other characters' Evotypes, like everyone else.
