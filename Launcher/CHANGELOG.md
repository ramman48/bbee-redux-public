# Nine the Phantom: changelog

## 0.9.0 alpha

Everything that changed since **0.7.2**, the last public build. Move names are the ones the game shows in English; [docs/NINE_GUIDE.md](docs/NINE_GUIDE.md) has her full move list.

### New moves and systems

- **Magic Arts.** Press Attack + Skill together to switch her Attack to her close-range strikes from BlazBlue Centralfiction, and press them again to switch back. It works on the ground and in the air, and the switch keeps the elements she holds.
  - Three strings: on the ground (ice, wind, fire), Down + Attack (wind, ice, fire) and in the air (fire, ice, wind). Each strike gives its element.
  - Every strike reaches across the screen with its own long effect: an ice lance, a gale line with crescents, a flame stream.
- **Elements are first in, first out.** When she holds three, a new element pushes out the oldest one.
- **One-element Weaves.** With a single element, Summon casts Lapis lazuli of Lamentation (ice), Emerald of Enmity (wind) or Coral of Catastrophe (fire). That makes 19 Weaves, one for every combination of one, two or three elements.
- **Garnet of Gall** is the new fire ×2 Weave: two spinning blades of flame close in on her target and explode where they meet.
- **Three SP moves.** Navy Pressure is joined by **Colorless Void** (Down + Skill, 100 SP) and **Flame Punisher** (Forward + Skill, 50 SP): a torrent of flame from her palm, then a spinning kick that fires a second, larger one.
- **Rising Roar** (air Up + Attack): the Phantom's arm sweeps up through a pillar of fire and launches enemies.
- **Seamoss Gate and the Stock Slot** (Forward + Summon): store the elements she holds, or swap them with the stored ones. The Summon slot shows what the stored elements would cast.
- **The Summon slot shows the Weave that is ready,** with its glyph and name.
- **Citrine of Change's mirror** now rebounds Amethyst of Annihilation's ball, faster, back through the enemies the mirror holds.
- **Flax Nurture** (Up + Attack) is a ground move only. In the air, Up + Attack is Rising Roar when you have it, and her air string when you don't.
- **Optional Centralfiction animations.** If BlazBlue Centralfiction is installed through Steam on the same account, two of her moves use her Centralfiction animations, read from your own copy. Without it nothing is missing: the same moves use her Entropy Effect animations.

### Potentials

- **37 Potentials, 11 of them new:** Crimson Raider, Elemental Burst, Unbroken Spire, Rising Roar, Scarlet Vein, Colorless Void, Flame Punisher, Burning Red, Seamoss Gate, Eightfold Blink and Phantom Ambush.
- **27 hidden bonuses:** holding both Potentials of a pair adds an extra effect.
- Every Potential now has a clear effect of its own.
- Burning Red raises the damage of her Weaves and her attacks, so her spells keep growing through a run.
- **Air Weaver** now really resets the limit of her air attacks.

### Balance

- She is stronger at high Entropy. Her weakest spells were raised: Kunzite of Keep Breaker, Amethyst of Annihilation, Cardinal Nova, Cobalt Hail, Ultramarine March, Celadon Lance and Cerulean Omen.
- **The fire rush** (the third hit of Vermilion Waltz) now deals its full damage. It dealt almost none.
- **Nephrite of Nullification, the arcane echo and nova, and Andradite of Accord's beams** now deal their intended damage. Andradite's beams also reach less far and hit each enemy a limited number of times per cast.
- **Citrine of Change's mirror** is much sturdier, lasts 8 seconds and cuts twice as it slides out.
- **Hit limits.** Amethyst of Annihilation could hit one enemy more than twenty times against a wall; it is now limited. Uvarovite of Undoing's storm can no longer hit one enemy more than about twenty times.
- **A Weave frees her sooner.** About a second into the cast, any input cancels the rest of the animation, and the spell carries on.
- Magic Arts strikes are quicker and lighter than her spell string: they trade damage for control over her elements.
- Her HP is 950.

### Fixes

- **Cerulean Omen** (the frost orb) now bursts once, on its target. It used to scatter bursts around itself and could miss.
- A Skill's elements are no longer lost when you dash right after pressing it.
- **Eightfold Blink** (Up + Dash, and Down + Dash in the air) and **Phantom Ambush** (Down + Dash on the ground) now work. Their inputs gave the plain blink.
- The air attack string is limited to once per jump, as described. **Rising Roar** resets when you land.
- The fire palm's hit now reaches as far as its flame.
- **Colorless Void:** all eight meteors now land on the bound enemy. Each one used to knock it out from under the next, so one in eight hit.
- **Phantom Ambush** now turns her to face the enemy she appears behind.
- **Mauvette Roar's** landing blast now connects.
- **Andradite of Accord** fired one volley and then hung on screen. Each orb now fires twice and leaves.
- **Coquelicot Spire** (hold Attack) no longer turns her around.
- Effects no longer stay behind after a cancelled move: the afterimage that followed her, a fire loop that never ended, casting circles and release flashes now stop when the move is interrupted.
- Her recoveries now play. Many casts, her blink, landings and get-ups were cut off before the animation finished. Lying down after a knockdown, she no longer bounces again and again.
- Forward + Summon can no longer fall through to a Weave.
- The intro-movie button on character select is switched off for her, as she has no intro movie.

### Presentation

- **Flame Punisher** has its own fire: orange flame gathers in her hands, a thin dark-violet lance leaves her palm, and the kick fires a swirling orange torrent. Both torrents reach across the screen.
- **Less clutter.** Every effect of hers was measured and scaled to a share of the view, and Nephrite of Nullification no longer dims the screen.
- **New icons.** Every Potential, her Skill button, her two Legacy skills, her talent and the 19 Weave glyphs were redrawn in the game's two-tone style.
- **Portraits.** Her character-select portrait is no longer squashed and is larger. She has her own art on the final-boss HUD, the profile hero list, the PvP result banner, the lobby player information and the co-op HUD, and her portraits sit correctly on every screen.
- Her talent icon shows in the battle HUD, and Potential icons there no longer blend into each other.
- **Idle animations and a second win pose.** Standing still, she now turns with her cape or looks back, as other characters do.

### Text and languages

- **Names from BlazBlue Centralfiction.** Moves with a Centralfiction counterpart take its official name: Flax Nurture (was Ice Globe), Mauvette Roar (was Meteor Heel), Navy Pressure (was Phantom Arm) and Cardinal Nova (was Element Gather). Every Weave is named after the Centralfiction spell of the same recipe; Grand Finale, for example, is now Nephrite of Nullification. Her Legacy skills are Rubellite of Rage and Tanzanite of Torment.
- Her other moves have names in the same style: Vermilion Waltz, Cerulean Omen, Coquelicot Spire, Viridian Scythe, Celadon Lance, Ultramarine March, Cobalt Hail and Verdigris Rebuff.
- Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean use the official Centralfiction names.
- Every new move, Potential and hidden bonus has its text in all 10 game languages.
- A second review of the nine translations fixed about 60 lines: grammar, the game's own terms and consistent names.
- Viridian Scythe's description now says what it does: it cuts across the whole screen, in front of her and behind.

### Not in this release

- Izanami, the unfinished preview in the 0.7.x packages, is not part of this release. The installer removes her data from your save after backing it up; Nine's data stays. See [docs/UPDATE.md](docs/UPDATE.md).
