DELTARUNE: Party, alpha TEST package: the pack folder

These are the DELTARUNE files Kris, Susie and Ralsei are built from on your PC: the sprites the mod uses, the sounds,
the font and the portrait artwork. They are here so that you can test the mod without owning DELTARUNE.

They belong to DELTARUNE's owners (Toby Fox and the DELTARUNE team; the portrait artwork is by Temmie Chang).
Please don't pass this folder on outside the package. A later release is to read the same files from your own copy of
DELTARUNE instead. If you like these three, play DELTARUNE.
