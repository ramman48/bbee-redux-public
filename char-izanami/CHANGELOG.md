# Izanami: patch notes

## 0.6.11 (2026-11-20)
- Fixed Dash Attack: the kick carries her forward again, and Flower Chain still lands after it.
- Fixed Hover after an air attack: Up + Jump goes into Hover after a missed air attack too, not only after a hit.
- Fixed Ribcage's look staying on her after the cage ended between stages. The look now ends with the cage.
- Fixed damage numbers showing on Ribcage while it was up. A blow on the cage now plays its own spark and sound.
- Fixed her art missing from the Save Evotype screen and her other portrait slots.
- Fixed SP: her hits fill SP while she holds Reaping of Yomi or Moment of Benevolence.
- Changed: she can equip other characters' Legacy skills and talents, like everyone else.
- Changed: her attacks keep going through light hits, as the other characters' do. Skills, finishers and Ultras hold through most hits, and every Skill is invincible while it winds up.
- Changed: a light hit stuns her for as long as it stuns other characters (it lasted almost twice as long).
- Changed: the tapped Meteor lands sooner (about 0.6 s after the press, was 1 s). Dash Strike and Yomi's Hook come out sooner.
- Changed: Max HP 800 to 1000, attack 170 to 190.
- Changed: lowering Ribcage early bursts it, as when it runs out.

## 0.6.5 (2026-11-14)
- Changed the wording of the Potential and move texts to read like the game's own. No move works differently.
- Fixed Ribcage showing a cost of 100 MP. It costs 30 MP.
- Fixed Eternal Darkness and Hour of Nihility showing an SP cost. They cost 100 MP and 50 MP.
- Fixed the inputs shown for Eternal Darkness, Hour of Nihility, Melancholy Death, Moment of Benevolence, Stake of Supremacy and Yomi's Hook.
- Fixed Yasakani's text saying Down + Summon lets it rest. Summon again lets it rest, and Down + Summon lays a Yasakani.
- The new wording is in all ten languages.
