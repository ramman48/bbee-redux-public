# Tactics Plus Ultra: patch notes

## 1.1.5 (2026-11-16, development)
- Fixed: Tide and Decay Tactics, their families and their descriptions showed names like BlessFaction_1101 and TriggerDesc_37112 when the game was not set to English or Chinese. They show the English text now.

## 1.1.3 (2026-11-14, development)
- Added: each Tide and Decay family has an emblem of its own in the Tactic Codex and the Tactic trees. They showed their first Tactic's icon before.

## 1.1.2 (2026-11-14, development)
- Fixed: the Tactic Codex wheel shows the Tide and Decay badges instead of the Ice and Toxin ones, and each Tide and Decay family in its list has its own icon.
- Fixed: LB and RB in Tactic Details now reach the Decay and Double tabs, and the RB prompt no longer covers the Decay tab.

## 1.1.1 (2026-11-11)
- Changed: Starting Tactics can be picked in the new Starting Tactics menu on the Legacy screen (Redux Menus 0.1.13). The older tree picker still opens when Redux Menus is switched off.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)
- Tactics Plus Ultra 1.0: the Tide and Decay release, as its own component of Redux. The content of 2.0.0-dev.23, renumbered.
- 65 Tactics (33 Tide, 32 Decay) across ten trees, each with its icon, its badge and its description, in the library, the Tactic trees, the reward cards and the battle HUD.
- Up to 4 starting Tactics picked on the Legacy screen before a run.
- Known issue: some Tide and Decay effects are early versions and will be replaced.
- Not in this release: the mixed-element Doubles (Tide or Decay with Fire, Ice and the others), Doppelganger and Overclock. Online co-op is untested.

## 2.0.0-dev.23 (2026-10-25)
- Fixed: the Cherenkov wisp now leaves you and flies to its target before it pulses.
- New large Tide and Decay badges (they showed the Ice badge).
- Dash Soak's description is shorter.
- Known issue: some Tide and Decay effects are early versions and will be replaced.

## 2.0.0-dev.22 (2026-10-25)
- Water and Radiation are now named Tide and Decay: 65 Tactics, 33 Tide and 32 Decay.
- New icons for every Tide and Decay Tactic, in the library, the Tactic trees, the reward cards and the battle HUD; new Tide and Decay badges.
- New Riptide tree: Dash Riptide, Dodge Swell, Counter Swell, Breaker and Rip Current.
- New Doubles: Surfing Whirlpool, Breakwater, Critical Beam and Wisp Core.
- Ten new Tactic trees, one per Tide and Decay family, under View Tactic Tree.
- Fixed: Dash Riptide's description showed the wrong Crash numbers.
- Reworked descriptions for all 65 Tactics. The longest ones (the Soak and Isotope starters, Tsunami) are shorter and easier to read.
- Mutagen, the Tide and Poison Double, is held back for a later update.
- Known issue: the Cherenkov wisp appears at its target instead of flying there.
- Known issue: the large Tide and Decay badge still shows the Ice badge.
- Known issue: some Tide and Decay effects are early versions and will be replaced.

## 2.0.0-dev.20 (2026-10-25)
- New Water trees: Geyser (your Legacy Skill launches enemies and they Crash when they land), Hydro Jet (a jet that stops at walls and can Ricochet) and Whirlpool (a Summon that pulls enemies in).
- New Water Doubles: Crashing Jet, Spike Down and Undertow Geyser.
- New Radiation trees: Gamma Ray (a ray from your Skill or Legacy that sweeps upward), Reactor (a core that pulses Decay and can melt down), Cherenkov Wisp (a wisp that pulses at the most irradiated enemy) and Fallout Step (your dash and Perfect Dodge leave a fallout trail).
- New Radiation Doubles: Reactor Beam, Fallout Fission and Gamma Trail.
- Fixed: a multi-hit Skill on one enemy now adds an Isotope for every hit.
- Fixed: Fission now splits an enemy's Isotopes when it dies.
- Fixed: Cascade now sends the wisp's pulse on when the pulsed enemy dies.
- Known issue: the Cherenkov wisp appears at its target instead of flying there.

## 2.0.0-dev.10.2 (2026-10-23)
- New: Starting Tactics. On the Legacy screen before a run, pick up to 4 Tactics from every Tactic tree and start the run with them, no Legacy needed.
- Your picks replace your Evotypes' Legacy Tactics for that run and start at the lowest quality. Remove them all to get your Legacy back.
- Heat that locks Legacy slots still applies: each locked slot drops two of your picks.
- Tactics that clash with one you already picked can't be added.

## 2.0.0-dev.10.1
- Fixed: Isotopes now stay on an enemy when you hit it, instead of being cleared by every hit.
