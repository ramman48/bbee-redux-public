# char-ruby: patch notes

## 1.0.7 (2026-11-20)
- Fixed the air Down + Dash stopping Ruby in mid-air once its push ended. She now keeps falling forward after it, the way the Up + Dash carries her up.

## 1.0.4 (2026-11-14)
- Changed the wording of the Potential and move texts to read like the game's own. No move works differently.
- The new wording is in all ten languages.

## 1.0.3 (2026-11-10)

Potentials
- Each family of Potentials shows one icon, the way the stock heroes' cards do.
- The Potentials page in a run lists every one of her moves again (it stopped after Reaper's Hook), and its damage line shows the damage icon instead of placeholder text. Recoil Shot is listed under Rifle Shot.
- Her hidden effects show in the move list and on the cards.
- The move list gives Dust's real price, 20 MP, instead of 25 SP.

Gun Blast
- Corkscrew Slice on the ground gives her blasts back, as any other attack does, so a blast, Corkscrew, blast loop no longer runs out after six.
- Holding Up-Forward and Dash on the ground with the Gun Blast card keeps blasting upward, as it already did in the air.
- An upward blast carries her up a little after its push instead of stopping dead.

Multiplayer
- In online fights her Dust now works (Fire bursts, Ice chills, freezes and shatters, Lightning arcs, Gravity opens its wells), and her power stages rise with her Potentials, so the effects that need a stage work there too.

Look
- Her eyes are open while she stands idle, and in the other frames that had them shut.
- Clash Assault's full-charge backdrop is much dimmer: its brightest moment is about 40% darker, and the white wash at its end is now a soft grey.

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-08)

Skills
- In the air, Skill with Forward held fires Recoil Shot straight ahead; with no direction it still fires diagonally down. Up and Down + Skill do what they did before.
- Guillotine's lunge now happens on its gunshot, and its first thrust stays active through it, so all seven cuts land on enemies at the far end of its reach instead of five or six.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-07)

Skills
- Guillotine and Reaper's Hook no longer zoom the camera in, slow time or darken the screen as they start, so they keep her pace. Their hits, hit-stop and screen shake are unchanged.
- The air Skill, Recoil Shot, now fires diagonally down and forward from Crescent Rose's barrel, so it reaches enemies below her. The recoil still carries her back and holds her in the air.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-01)

Supers
- Red Reaper now plays Cross Tag Battle's own cutscene with the enemy Ruby hit. The stage and the other enemies drop away, instead of a moon picture appearing over the stage.
- Petal Burst and Petal Dance are cleaned up. The white card, the hard-edged bands and the stray slashes are gone, and every slash now follows her swing.

Skills
- Guillotine and Reaper's Hook hit with the hit-stop and screen shake of the cast's own heavy hits and grabs.
- Guillotine's spin ring sits around her hips while she spins, and its last slash lands at body height instead of on the floor.
- Buzzsaw Blast plays like ICEY's Deadly Disc. Up launches her, then she dives; Down is a steady dive. It costs 40 MP once, its ring closes into a full circle, and the scythe swing no longer shows as she lands.
- Skills can be used right after Buzzsaw Blast lands, instead of about 3 seconds later. Dash cancels it on a hit with any number of blasts left.
- Holding Skill goes from Rifle Shot straight into the Sy-Pod plant, without freezing in the shot's pose.
- A held Sy-Pod fires its first round right away.

Gun Blast
- Tap Dash for a short blast, hold it to go farther.
- Gun Blast card: keep Dash held to keep firing and blasting the same way, 3 blasts in a row, or 6 with the chain card. This replaces the long-range blast card.
- The chain card now chains 6 blasts, in any order and direction.
- Directions fixed: Down-Forward on the ground blasts forward, Up-Back turns her and blasts up and back, and Up-Forward in the air blasts up.
- Attacking between blasts gives the blasts back, so she no longer has to stand still after three.
- Each blast is invincible for its first 0.2 seconds.
- Every dash and blast shows a red ring, turned the way she goes.

In the air
- Air Attack and the second air hit keep her momentum instead of stopping her.
- Her air finisher ends into her fall instead of holding its last frame until she lands.
- The air finisher's rounds now point along their diagonal flight.
- New Potential, Airborne Attack: the air finisher fires 6 rounds instead of 3, and holding Attack in the air goes straight into it. Its icon is a placeholder until its own is drawn.

On the ground
- The fourth basic hit no longer launches the enemy or makes Ruby jump.

Dust
- Dust is now her ammunition. One element is loaded at a time, changing it costs 20 MP, and each element changes what her rounds do.
- Gravity rounds open a well that pulls enemies in, then collapses.
- New effects for each element's change and for its hits. Changing Dust no longer fires a round, and Gravity's reload plays once.
- Retired: Dust Round, and the Dust card that made the spent cartridge explode.

Look and sound
- Only her eyes, her emblem and buckles and her blade's edge glow; her outline no longer does. Each palette glows in its own colours.
- New Potential, skill and talent icons, her own crest, and her colour palettes.
- Her own backdrop in the dev menu's test room.
- Fewer voice lines, idle ones especially.

Other
- Stagger no longer interrupts bosses and Elites; they still take its damage.
