# char-weiss: patch notes

## 0.4.13 (2026-11-20)

From Boogy's report on 0.4.12 (every bug, the text notes and the design requests).

Fixed
- Her idle breathing plays through the whole stand instead of stopping after one breath.
- The Crush Trigger can't be used again straight after a miss: a missed leap has its landing and a short pause first.
- Heavy Thrust has Super Armor as its text says, instead of making her invincible.
- The fourth strike of her Attack string crashes a black glyph onto the enemy whenever the glyph is in range, not only when she stands next to it.
- The Arma Gigas's sword hits enemies on either side of it, including enemies behind her.
- Snowfall's held landing hits only what its cuts reach, and with Frost the enemy stays frozen through the rising cuts.
- Her first air attack hits through its whole swing, including enemies below her.
- Jumping off a black glyph no longer uses up her double jump.
- Glyph Throw comes out when Down + Dash is pressed during a dash, right after one, or twice in a row.
- The Long Lunge hold carries the dash attack farther, with a slide to a stop.
- Triple Strike holds the enemy in front of her, so all three cuts and the finisher land. The dash attack now staggers the enemy and slides it along instead of knocking it down, so what follows can reach it.
- The dash attack's glyph (Lunge Glyph) appears on the hit, also when a direction is held or a follow-up comes right after.
- Fire Dust's burst and burning floor come with its Potential only; without it the fireball hits on its own.
- Lightning Dust's circuit between her glyphs comes with its Potential only, and it now damages every enemy on it.
- Wind Dust's Potential opens a vortex where a tornado first hits.
- Fire, Lightning and Wind shots have sounds.
- Ice Pillar's walls stop enemies, no longer break when an enemy walks into them, and break from enemy hits instead. They are narrower and taller, with no ice spikes behind them.
- Frost Pillar's ice pillar rises from the ground under the enemy instead of in the air.
- White Night and Glacial Torrent are Ultras now, so her SP gauge fills in a run.
- The hidden effect that gives Super Armor during the Crush Trigger is now called Crush Trigger.

Changed
- Her first two Attack strikes can go into the Up + Attack or Down + Attack string, her third into that string's last hit, and every ground string ends with the fourth strike. The Down + Attack string can't restart itself.
- Snowfall dives at once, in a fast plunge.
- A black glyph lights up and grows when she is in range to use it, and the range is as large above the glyph as beside it. It is the same range for her follow-ups, Glyph Cancel, the fourth strike and jumping off it.
- Jump cancels her air attacks early, as her dash does.
- Glyph Step also leaves a glyph under her third jump.
- The dash attack ends without its long recovery.
- Glyph Dash: during a dash, Up + Attack, Up + Skill or Up + Dash at a black glyph gives Twisting Step, Piercing Step or Pirouette, with no Potential needed. Glissade now keeps the glyph's uses for those follow-ups.
- Glyph Cancel also works after the Down + Attack string's finisher and the dash attack.
- Piercing Step costs 20 MP, deals more damage and lands into Snowfall's landing strike and its follow-ups.
- Twisting Step springs up higher and cuts down in an arc. Twisting Step and Pirouette each leave a black glyph where they end for 2 seconds.
- Dust: Summon alone loads Ice, Up + Summon loads Fire.
- Precision Parry costs 20 MP, and Counter Glyph places its glyph just above her.
- Ice Pillar: Pillar Glyph places its glyph next to her. With Great Pillar the larger wall costs 75 MP and no hit breaks it.
- Glacial Torrent is Up + Skill with its own Potential (where Snow Globe was), and costs 50 SP. Holding Attack is always Heavy Thrust.
- EX Ice Shard places one black glyph in front of the nearest enemy and one behind it, wherever it stands on the screen.
- Glyph Runner gives Super Armor for 1 second after a dash starts.
- Many move and Potential texts reworded as Boogy asked. The changed lines show in English in the other nine languages until they are translated.

Removed
- Snow Globe and its Potential (Glacial Torrent takes its place; Globe Glyph became Torrent Glyph).

## 0.4.7 (2026-11-13)

From the testers' reports of 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13.

Changed
- Weiss can have at most three glyphs out at once. Glyphs from her Potentials count toward this, including EX Ice Shard's two, the glyph under her double jump and the glyph her lunge leaves. A fourth glyph replaces the oldest one, which breaks apart as it normally does when it expires.
- A glyph appears at full size when she casts it. After about half a second it shrinks to 70% of that size and loses its outer glow, so it covers less of the enemy. A black glyph in range of her dash returns to full size and pulses, as before.
- Third Glyph and Potential Unleashed: each now lets all three of her glyphs be the same colour. Without either card she can have at most two of one colour.

Fixed
- The fourth hit of her Attack string turns her toward a held direction as it starts and never slides her.

## 0.4.6 (2026-11-13, development)

From the lead, after trying 0.4.5.

Changed
- Ice Pillar: Down twice, then Attack raises a pillar about half as tall as before; Down twice, then Skill raises a larger one. Every pillar of hers now shatters when it breaks: a flash, a burst of ice and chunks that fall away. This covers Glacial Torrent, Snow Globe and White Night's pillars too.
- Snowfall's landing no longer raises an ice pillar. With Frost it still freezes what it hits.
- Hold Attack is a charge attack, timed like Noel's: hold from 0.4 seconds, she lunges and thrusts and can't be knocked out of the wind-up; press Attack again for the rising thrust. Glacial Torrent comes out only if you keep holding Attack through the thrust with 50 SP, so a charge attack you let go of stays a charge attack.
- Crush Trigger (Down + hold Attack) works like Ruby's Clash Assault: she leaps in and strikes, lands, and on a hit chases the enemy down with her flurry of cuts over Cross Tag's colour-burst backdrop. Holding the button no longer starts it twice.
- Arma Gigas: the sword aims at the enemy and swings like a giant blade, lifting back before each cut, sweeping through, overshooting and settling. Each cut leaves a trail along the sword's own swing. The fifth cut throws a flying ice wave level along the floor. At rest the sword sways and floats. No more ice crystals or spikes coming off it.
- Her Potentials are grouped into 13 rows at the bottom of the screen instead of one row per card.
- Her tiara, the pendant at her neck, the heels of her boots, Myrtenaster's Dust chamber and its blade glow.
- Four palettes: Black Ice, Crimson Waltz, Gilded Frost and Violet Glyph, each with its own glow colours.
- Triple Strike (Dash, Attack, Attack) is faster and drawn: its first cut lands about 0.18 seconds after the press instead of 0.4, the three cuts come in quick succession, and each has its own slash, the last one a large rising cut.
- Arma Gigas's sword no longer glows or flickers; it is a pale blade that sways at rest.

Fixed
- Glacial Torrent no longer drops back into a crouch after she has risen; the move ends as she stands.
- The fourth hit of her Attack string: holding a direction no longer slides her while she stands still; it only turns her. Its ice stays on the floor when she jumps right after it.
- Her Potential page lists every group again (Heavy Thrust, the Crush Trigger and Glyph Cancel sit under their moves).

Removed
- Snow Flurry (Up + hold Skill) and its Potential Snow Flurry II. White Night keeps that input.

From the lead: "foward plus skill move and its follow ups they all need major reworks visually from scratch again the glyphs are fine but the slash and support vfxing need to match the quality of offical entropy effect characters".

- Her glyphs are unchanged.

From the lead: "her moves look bad and weak and lackluster and it just doesnt stand out at all ... she needs a total vfx overhaul".

## 0.4.1 (2026-11-10)

From the playtesters' feedback.

Added
- Three new hidden effects: Frozen Veil (Piercing Step + Frost), Storm Veil (Piercing Step + Snowstorm) and Shard Veil (Piercing Step + Glyph Burst). With any of them, Snowfall (air Down + Skill) makes her Invincible from its start until its landing ends, Snowstorm and Frost included.
- New hidden effect, Glyph Echo (Twisting Step + Lunge Glyph): the glyphs Twisting Step, Glyph Dash Strike and her double jump leave behind last 6 seconds instead of 2.

Changed
- Holding Skill for a glyph: the glyph appears the moment the cast turns into the summon, so she can attack, jump or dash right after without losing it. A tapped Skill's white glyph also comes out a little sooner.
- Snow Flurry and Glacial Torrent: she is Invincible for the whole move, as other characters are during their supers.
- Heavy Thrust (held Attack without the SP) and its rising second thrust: she is Invincible from the start of each thrust until it hits.
- Fire Dust hits less hard and no longer stuns: the fireball and its burst deal less, and the burning floor burns without making enemies flinch (its Burn is unchanged).

Fixed
- Encore: Twisting Step and Piercing Step can follow themselves (and each other) again after a step has carried her away from the glyph, as long as the glyph has uses left.
- With Snowstorm and Frost, holding Skill through Snowfall now erupts in ice (and freezes) before the rising cuts. It used to give Snowstorm alone.
- Her Dust goes back to Ice at the start of every run. A run abandoned on Fire Dust used to start the next run on Fire Dust.
- A black glyph made on the floor right after a white glyph placed in the air now stays at her floor height instead of appearing up at the white glyph's height.

## 0.3.3 (2026-11-07)

From the playtesters' feedback.

Fixed
- The dash attack (Dash, then Attack) hits enemies right beside her and enemies on the floor. Its hit now starts as she lunges and reaches back along her path.
- Down + Attack's second hit lands when you play the whole string. A quick third press used to cut it off before it struck.
- Twisting Step, Piercing Step and Pirouette work with Encore: holding the buttons no longer restarts the move over and over and breaks the glyph. They also work after the dash attack now, as its move list says.
- The first air attack no longer knocks the enemy upward, so the second one connects.
- The second and third air attacks only hit where she swings. They used to reach below her and too far ahead.
- The air string starts again at the first attack after a jump.
- Snowfall comes out from anywhere in the air.
- Forward + Skill's second hit carries the enemy up with her.

Changed
- Jump cancels: her ground and air Attack moves can be cancelled into a jump right after they hit, or late in a move that misses.
- She falls more gently through her air attacks, and after her follow-ups and Forward + Skill.
- The held Attack and Down + held Attack are swapped. The held Attack is now Glacial Torrent when you have 50 SP; without the SP it is Heavy Thrust, and pressing Attack again gives the rising second thrust. The Crush Trigger and its flurry are on Down + held Attack.
- Snowfall is air Down + Skill and costs 20 MP (the move list now says so). Plain Skill in the air is her Ice Shard, and holding it places a black glyph in the air.
- Precision Parry is Down + Skill on the ground and still costs no MP; keep Skill held and it becomes the far glyph. Down, Down + Dash is a normal dash again (or Glyph Throw with that card).
- Forward + Skill's last hit leaves the enemy in reach, and you can jump-cancel it to continue in the air.
- Glyph Throw (Down + Dash): the first hit pushes the enemy forward, and the second throws it forward and high. The second hit's reach is larger, so it always connects.
- Triple Strike (Dash, Attack, Attack): if the first cut misses, she stops there.
- Frost Pillar: one pillar per shot, at the first enemy it hits (or where the arrow lands). It used to raise one at every enemy.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-03)

Weiss Schnee from BlazBlue Cross Tag Battle, in her first test build. She fights with glyphs: she places them, fires from them and steps off them.

Glyphs
- Skill fires an Ice Shard. Hold it to place a black glyph instead.
- Down + held Skill places a black glyph farther ahead and higher, for air combos, with or without SP.
- Near a black glyph, during Glyph Dash Strike (Forward + Skill), Triple Strike, Snowfall or another follow-up: Up + Attack is Twisting Step, Up + Skill is Piercing Step, Up + Dash is Pirouette. Each takes one of the glyph's three uses.
- Follow-ups, strikes and glyph shots fill her SP. Up + held Skill with a full SP bar is Snow Flurry.

Dust
- Summon picks her Dust: tap for Fire, Down + Summon for Lightning, hold for Wind, Up + Summon to go back to Ice.
- Fire bolts burst where they land and leave the floor burning for 3 seconds.
- Lightning strikes the nearest enemy and jumps to two more, holding each in Paralysis for a moment.
- Wind gusts pull enemies in, then a vortex gathers them and throws them up.

Power stages
- As she collects Potentials, her follow-ups, Snowfall and Ice Pillar grow in two stages, up to screen-wide at stage 3. They hit harder and stop the hit for longer each stage.

Cards
- Twisting Step's card carries her farther and leaves a glyph where she ends. Piercing Step's card makes her invincible from its start until she lands.
- Snow Globe now goes on into Snow Flurry or White Night when Skill is held with a full SP bar.
- Ice Pillar: Down twice, then Attack for the wall; Down twice, then Skill for the larger wall. Precision Parry is Down twice, then Dash.

Known in this build
- The Dust effects are new and still being checked. Tell us if any of them look wrong, cover the enemy or are hard to see.
- Wind's pull strength and Fire's burn damage have not been tuned yet: say whether they feel too strong or too weak.
- Her Potential and skill icons are stand-ins until her own are drawn.
