# Nine: patch notes

## 1.0.10 (2026-11-20)
- Fixed the air string doing the fire palm twice before the tornado. In a jump, Attack now gives the palm, the tornado and then the fire rush.
- Fixed Attack + Skill pressed together in the middle of Vermilion Waltz not switching Magic Arts. It now switches on any press of any Attack string.
- Fixed Nine standing up for a moment between crouched attacks while Down is held. She now stays crouched.
- Changed Magic Arts' text to list its three orders of strikes one per line, and Weave's text to mark each spell with a dot, in all ten languages.

## 1.0.9 (2026-11-15)
- Fixed Rising Roar dealing damage only at the bottom of the Phantom's arm. The arm and the flame now hit along their whole length.
- Fixed Rising Roar's flame pointing down and backward when Nine faces left.
- Fixed Rising Roar playing Crimson Raider's sound. It now has the Phantom's own strike line.
- Fixed Navy Pressure's two kicks with Scarlet Vein landing at once behind the Phantom's hands. They now come one after the other once the hands are gone.
- Fixed Navy Pressure's kick missing enemies that were far away, small, or below Nine when she cast it in the air.
- Fixed Cobalt Hail giving only 1 ice spell with Elemental Mastery. It gives 1 fire spell and 1 ice spell again.
- Fixed Cobalt Hail with Hailstorm dropping 3 meteors with 2 fire spells and 1 ice spell held. It now drops 6.
- Fixed Viridian Scythe passing over enemies on the ground when cast from a high jump.
- Fixed Cerulean Omen's freezing area hanging flat in mid-air when the orb burst above the floor. It now lies on the floor.
- Fixed ice streaks spraying out of Cerulean Omen's orb.
- Fixed Flax Nurture's second globe with Twin Globe not looking like a globe. Both globes are now the same globe at the same size.
- Fixed the air string ending before the fire rush. In the air its last press is now the fire rush, or Crimson Raider with its card.
- Fixed the upward Blink from the ground leaving no frost trail with Phantom Step.
- Changed Weave's text to list every spell combination on its own line.
- Changed Hailstorm, Elemental Mastery and Phantom Step's texts to match what they do, in all ten languages.

## 1.0.8 (2026-11-14)
- Changed the wording of the Potential and move texts to read like the game's own. No move works differently.
- Fixed Seamoss Gate's input missing part of its button.
- Fixed card texts that still called her spells "elements".
- The new wording is in all ten languages.
