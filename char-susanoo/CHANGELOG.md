# Susanoo: patch notes

## 0.1.14 (2026-11-20)
- Strike of the Possessed God now fires when you hold Summon. Tapping Summon, for example to pick an option, no longer spends SP on it. Keep holding to power it up with MP as before.
- The beam can be started in the air: Susanoo now stays in the air while it fires and falls after it.
- Dash now cancels the beam once it fires, and comes out sooner during Sundering Claws, Dancing Dual Kick, his Skills and Aerial Claws.
- Liberating Dagger now cuts enemies behind Susanoo as well as ahead. Its invincibility ends once the cut lands, and Dash can cancel it after that.
- Roar of the Mad King gives control back right after the finishing blow. Before, he stood still for about 5 more seconds.
- Susanoo now gains less SP from each hit (attacks 0.6, Skills 2, from 1.5 and 4). Defeated enemies, Retribution and his Potentials give the same SP as before.
- His SP seal now shows next to the health bar from the start of a run, without needing EX Hakumen.
- Jump now recovers from being knocked away, as with the other heroes' Triple Jump.
- Holding Skill now takes a moment longer before Blade of Judgement or Retribution's guard comes out, so a slow tap stays Void Eruption.
- Void Charge now shows a burst of flame when it is fully charged.
- Void pillars and flame pillars now deal 2.5 times their damage, and void lines and orbs 1.5 times theirs.
- Sundering Claws, Dancing Dual Kick, Hunter's Fang's landing, the third Aerial Claw, Void Charge, Void Rend's last strikes and his Skills deal more damage. The beam's first phase deals twice as much, and Roar of the Mad King almost twice as much. Liberating Dagger deals about a quarter less.
- Flame pillars and Sundering Claws' last hit now break Super Armor, and bosses and elites can be staggered for 2 seconds in every 5 (before, 1.2 seconds in every 6.2).
- Only Roar of the Mad King, the beam and Liberating Dagger move the camera in now, every other move shakes the screen less, and Void Charge no longer slows time.
- Void pillars are about a third as tall and narrower, and flame pillars are smaller and end as soon as they finish.
- Blade of Judgement's flaming sword no longer stays on Susanoo for about 3 seconds after the cut.
- He runs at Hakumen's speed, and his run animation keeps pace with his feet. Void Step glides farther and runs straight on when you hold a direction.

## 0.1.13 (2026-11-15)
- Fixed Susanoo's Void Rend Legacy drawing a line across the whole screen when another hero uses it.
- His other Legacy's void pillars rise as tall columns.

## 0.1.12 (2026-11-15)
- Each void pillar rises as a tall column instead of a pile of rocks, and the whole line of pillars rises when an enemy is near.
- Sky Render's flame pillars appear when Susanoo lands during his third air claw.
- The line from Void Rend's last hit runs only ahead of Susanoo, as far as it hits.
- Void Eruption's two circling orbs circle Susanoo and hit instead of staying under the floor.
- He gets back up from a knockdown as soon as other heroes do.

## 0.1.11 (2026-11-15)
- His Potential icons show on the Potential choice screen instead of white squares.

## 0.1.10 (2026-11-14)
- New Potential icons with the game's own figure.

## 0.1.9 (2026-11-14)
- Changed the wording of the Potential and move texts to read like the game's own. No move works differently.
- Fixed Triple Dash naming Hakumen's dash as the move it changes.
- Removed MP and SP costs from texts where the cost is already shown beside the move.
- The new wording is in all ten languages.

## 0.1.8 (2026-11-14)
- Susanoo's Skills and Ultras now give him super armour and halve the damage he takes while he casts them. Enemy hits no longer knock him out of a cast, and the protection ends as soon as the cast ends or is cancelled.
- The fire his dash, Towering Flame and Void Eruption leave on the ground is now green only, with fewer and lower flames. The black flames that drew over enemies are gone, so enemies standing in the fire stay easy to see.
- Towering Flame's pillars no longer wash the screen in green as they rise.
- Hunter's Fang can no longer be used without its Potential. Before, holding Dancing Dual Kick alone let you follow the kick with Hunter's Fang.
- Blade of Judgement can no longer be used without its Potential. Before, Splintering Thrust alone let you follow the thrust with it.
- The Potential Unleashed card now has its own name and description in every language, even when EX Hakumen is not installed.

## 0.1.7 (2026-11-10)
- Online and co-op: Roar of the Mad King no longer swings every player's camera onto Susanoo. Its camera focus and the slow-motion beats of his screen cuts, Liberating Dagger and the beam now play only when he fights alone.
- Online and co-op: the dark backdrop behind his screen cuts, Roar and beam shows only on his own player's screen, so teammates keep seeing the stage.
- Online and co-op: Strike of the Possessed God's held beam follows your Teammate Effect Intensity setting when a teammate fires it: Off hides phases 2 and 3, Low draws them at phase 1's size.
- Void Step comes out in the middle of a combo: Dash cancels his Attack string from just after each blow starts, and a Dash pressed a moment early is kept.

## 0.1.6 (2026-11-07)
- Installing the Redux set through the Terminal no longer stops at Susanoo: his package now carries the pictures his icons are built from.
- His two Legacy skills and his talent have their own icons instead of Hakumen's.

## 0.1.5 (2026-11-05)
- Susanoo no longer needs EX Hakumen. His moves, pillars, beam, Legacy skills, talent and gauge are all his own now, so he can be installed and played on his own, and changes to EX Hakumen no longer affect him.
- The Megalith pillars no longer dim the whole screen as they rise.
- In the held Strike of the Possessed God, phases 2 and 3 now start from his mouth in the crouched pose instead of above his head.

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-05)
- Void Step can no longer be chained without end. He steps once, then waits a moment, like every other character; with the Triple Dash Potential, which is now in his pool, he steps three times and then waits a little longer. The same goes for his air dash.
- Void Step makes him invincible only for its first instant now, as the other characters' dashes do. Dashing through an attack at the right moment counts as a Perfect Dodge again.
- Vanishing Step shares Void Step's wait, so the two can no longer be traded back and forth without a pause.
- In the air, Vanishing Step works once per jump again. After it he falls instead of standing in mid-air.
- Megalith: holding Skill no longer stops the stones from rising. Every stone rises first, then he takes Retribution's guard. Pressing another button during Megalith no longer cancels the last stones.
- Strike of the Possessed God: pressing Attack or Dash as the beam starts no longer cancels it and wastes the SP. Holding Summon reaches the next phase more reliably.
- Splintering Thrust: pressing Skill any time during the thrust or the slam now follows with Blade of Judgement when you have both cards. It costs 50 MP like a Blade of Judgement on its own.
- Liberating Dagger comes out every time Summon is pressed during an attack, even pressed together with Attack. Before, a quick press often fired the beam instead.
- Void Step: the second Down + Dash is accepted from just after the first until shortly after he reappears, instead of only in a narrow moment.
- Down + Summon plays Susanoo's summon pose with the Summon Tactic instead of leaving him crouched.
- Landing from a fall through a platform shows Susanoo instead of Hakumen.
- Void Charge's charging sounds stop when the charge is cancelled.
- Leftover input rows of Hakumen's own moves are gone.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-05)
- His Legacy skills, Void Pillars and Susanoo's Rend, and his talent, Magatama, now show their names and descriptions on every screen. Their icons are placeholders for now.
- Void Feed, Retribution, Roar of the Mad King, Strike of the Possessed God, Eternal Void, Void Hunger, World Cutter and Endless Hunger are now translated into every language. Before, these lines were in English everywhere.
- Susanoo no longer changes when EX Hakumen is updated.
- Works with game update 26424974.

## 0.1.2 (2026-10-23)
- Starting a run as Susanoo from the lobby no longer locks him in place. His entrance pose there never ended, so he could not walk to the door and the run never began.
- The scene at the start of a run where the Legacy drops in now shows Susanoo instead of Hakumen. So do the revive and the other stage scenes.
- Strike of the Possessed God: holding Summon goes through all three phases again when his MP is above 100. Before, a boon that raised his MP past 100 kept the beam at phase one.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-23)
- He starts every run and every room as Susanoo, and character select shows Susanoo instead of Hakumen.
- His SP seal now shows next to the health bar.
- Roar of the Mad King, Liberating Dagger, Towering Flame, Megalith and Dance of the Dual Kick work again.
- Megalith levels 2 and 3 now add their extra stones.
- With Retribution, tapping Down + Skill on the ground gives Megalith again; holding it still gives the guard. Before, an ordinary tap counted as a hold, so Megalith seemed to work only in the air.
- Void Hunger raises a stone where an enemy dies.
- Void Step's "twice before landing" works: press Down + Dash again during the first Vanishing Step.
- In the air, Vanishing Step works once per jump (twice with Void Step) until he lands. Before, it could be chained without end.
- His guard (Down + Skill held) works facing left as well as right.
- The beam fires the way he faces and keeps that direction.
