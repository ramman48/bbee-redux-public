# Entropy Overhaul: Grim of the Abyss: patch notes

## 1.0.5 (2026-11-14)
- Added: the twenty new Mind Crystals have icons of their own. They wore the icons of other crystals before.

## 1.0.4 (2026-11-14)
- Removed: the Endless tab. Entropy Overhaul has Grim of the Abyss alone for now; Endless comes back in a later update.

## 1.0.3 (2026-11-14)
- Fixed: with a controller, the Entropy menu briefly jumped to a neighbouring Mutation or modifier on each move, and pushing the stick right could pick the row above or below.
- Fixed: on a double-reward room, the x2 mark stayed bright white when the room was not the one picked, covering its reward icon. It now dims with the rest of the row and lights up when the row is picked.
- Fixed: with two Champions up at once, their health bars sat so close that the names ran into each other. The bars now sit farther apart, and a long name is cut short to fit its own bar.

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-13)
- Added: Entropy Presets. On the Extreme, Grim and Endless tabs of the Entropy menu, press R3 (R on a keyboard) to open them. They work like Mind Crystal groups: Save puts your current selection into a slot, Use loads it back, and you can rename, copy or clear a slot. Presets are kept on your PC, apart from your save.
- Fixed: with the Glass Cannon Mutation, your HP could drop below zero after the first room. Max HP now stays at its locked 150%.
- Fixed: Charged Environment's lightning hit you during a dash or a Skill's invincibility. A strike now passes over anyone who is invincible, as an enemy attack does.
- Changed: Charged Environment's strikes are easier to see. The marked spot flashes a lightning warning through the 1.5 seconds before the strike, a bolt comes down from the sky onto it, and everyone it hits shows a spark.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-10)
- Changed: making Par Time gives Max HP or Max MP at random, shown in the banner. There is no pop-up to confirm any more, so nothing covers the next choice.
- Fixed: the dev menu's God mode now also protects you from Charged Environment's lightning.
- Changed: Charged Environment's warning lasts 1.5 seconds (was 1) and its red floor band is stronger.
- Fixed: in co-op, each player's game could roll its own lightning spots, Champion traits, summons and Endless wave picks, so the players saw different fights (lightning hitting only one player, strikes missing). Every player's game now rolls the same.
- Fixed: a Champion's health bar showed a code like "Monster_350701" instead of a name. Champions without a name of their own are now called Abyss Champion.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)
- Grim of the Abyss and Endless are finished: the content of 0.14.4, released as 1.0.0.

## 0.14.4 (2026-10-25)
- An Endless run no longer makes the next run with the same character Endless after a restart.
- Champion's Due no longer fails after starting a new run in the same session.
- Champions rank I now shows its correct text in all ten languages.
- Wellspring now gives its MP recovery, and Par Time counts every room it should.
- Abyss ranks rebalanced: enemy damage grows less at the top ranks and enemy health grows more; Endless gets a ninth
  tier, and "More standard enemies" gets a rank IV on the Grim and Endless tabs.

## 0.14.0 (2026-10-23)
- Fifteen new Mind Crystals, unlocked once Extreme is, like the three of 0.13.0. Their icons are placeholders for now.
  - Abyss crystals, which work only in Grim of the Abyss and Endless: Abyss Pact (more damage for each Abyss rank), Abyss Ward (less damage taken for each Abyss rank), Deep Diver (more damage for each stage), Warden's Bane (Champions and Warded Elites take more damage) and Champion's Due (beating a Champion heals you).
  - Risk and reward: Glass Cannon, Blood Price (hits heal you, you deal less damage), Overclock (faster attacks, slower Legacy Skills), Spellblade (stronger Skills, weaker Attacks) and Iron Shell (less damage taken, slower attacks).
  - Build shaping: Legacy Surge (faster Legacy Skills), Keen Edge (Critical Rate and Damage), Purist (more damage the more Tactics you hold from one faction), Collector (more damage for each faction you hold) and Wellspring (faster MP recovery).

## 0.13.1 (2026-10-23)
- Fixed: the three new Mind Crystals showed "Locked" with no condition and could not be unlocked. They now unlock like Terminal Predator: once Extreme is unlocked, for their 500 cost. Saves that already have Extreme unlocked get them on the next start of the game.

## 0.13.0 (2026-10-23)
- Three new Mind Crystals: Quickened Tempo (Attack Speed +30/40/50%), Swift Tactics (all Tactic cooldowns -15/20/25%) and Deep Reserves (Max SP +20/30/40, for heroes with an SP gauge). Their icons are placeholders for now.
- Fixed: online Endless played at Grim difficulty. Every player in a co-op run now plays the host's mode, Grim and Endless included, even before unlocking it.
- Fixed: the boss label said Grim in an Extreme lobby.
- Fixed: the boss buff marker kept cycling during the cutscene between Takemikazuchi and Izanami.
- Abyssal Insight's text now says what it does: each Elite Enemy beaten on stages 4 and 5 earns one more Mind Unleashed effect, offered at the next reward.
